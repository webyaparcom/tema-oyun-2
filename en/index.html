Questions

Before you press start

Turn the deck with the arrows or by swiping.

  1. 1 / 6sports_esports

    What kind of games do you make?

    Puzzle, calm exploration and platform games. All are drawn in pixel art, with no harsh language or crude scenes.

  2. 2 / 6handshake

    Do you work on other studios’ games?

    Yes; we also take on pixel art, sound design and game testing on their own.

  3. 3 / 6graphic_eq

    Where do you record the sound?

    In the small recording room in the workshop; most effects come from everyday objects.

  4. 4 / 6accessibility_new

    Do your games have accessibility settings?

    Every game has a colour-blind palette, text size and key remapping.

  5. 5 / 6school

    Are there internships or volunteer testing?

    We post internships at the start of each term; a monthly open playtest evening is open to everyone.

  6. 6 / 6info

    Are the games on this site real?

    Sekizbit is a fictional demo game workshop; game titles, figures and people are samples and images are representative.
